10 tips for finding information on the Internet

1) Determine the nature of the information. Determine the nature of the information. News? It may be copied to other websites. File? It can be uploaded to other hosting/FTP. A page on social media? Could have remained in the network indexers.

2) Gather/recall as much information as possible about the search subject. Page title, exact unique text snippets or links, full name, file size or other properties (for example, exact size, MD5 hash).

3) The first step in searching is Google. Never forget to refine your query! The simple addition of site:vk.com will show results only from VK, but you can also refine by date, page title, file type, and much more!

4) Didn't help? Didn't help? There are always thousands of other search engines and indexers, each differing from the others. The same Yandex better covers the Runet and is more accurate in morphology. [Here is a list of some search tools](https://cryptpad.fr/pad/#/1/view/V078jgnX5qtRhTlRIXvSAg/VahhfscJu7+Sf-74jS8LRNr6ewCLgBJ8nTcRLxdhSqE) (to be updated).

5) Something was found, but the link has already been deleted? Something was found, but the link is already deleted? Look at the cached version in the search engine! This is essentially a temporary file created by the search engine to remember and retrieve information. And don't forget that there are websites specifically for saving copies of pages: web.archive.org, archive.is, social media sites, image boards, etc.

6) Is the main site not indexed? Check if it has a mobile version and if it doesn't have an RSS/Sitemap! The mobile version of the site may have indexing that the main version does not, and the XML feeds may contain links and remaining information that was once removed from the main page.

7) Do you need to find a picture? Need to find a picture? Use Google Images and Yandex Images, there are also alternatives like TinEye. For searching for people by photo on social networks, there are services like Findface (for VK).

8) Currently, previews are implemented in many places - in VK and Telegram, when posting a link, an image and part of the text from the page are loaded. Sometimes this is enough to clarify the search data and find what you need.

9) Don't give up if you haven't found anything with obvious queries. Don't give up if you didn't find anything with obvious queries. Often, just a bit of analysis can help you understand which related queries can lead you to the desired information.

10) Stuck in your search? There are almost always people who have saved copies of the pages. Don't shy away from communication to obtain information.
